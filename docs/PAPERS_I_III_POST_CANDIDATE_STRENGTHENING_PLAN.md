## Manuscript integration freeze protocol

Before submission of Papers I–III perform exactly one controlled post-Candidate-development audit.

For each later result ask:

1. Does it change a scientific claim inside the frozen scope of Paper I, II, or III?
2. Does it supply a materially stronger theorem/counterexample directly relevant to that scope?
3. Is the result sufficiently mature and independently reproducible to cite?
4. Has novelty/priority been checked against current literature before labeling it novel?

Decision rule:

- **YES, material to frozen claim:** integrate and document why the paper was reopened.
- **YES, useful but not claim-changing:** add at most a concise Discussion/Outlook item.
- **NO:** leave it for Paper IV/comparator/Candidate-Gravity work.

After this audit, freeze manuscript scope. Do not continuously back-propagate every subsequent model iteration into Papers I–III.

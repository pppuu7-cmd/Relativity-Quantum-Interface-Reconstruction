## Project negative results suitable for a Results/Methods section

### CG-NG-003 — reference degeneracy

Standard perturbative quantum GR cannot be promoted as a new model when C5 is already the comparator. This establishes that quantizing the weak-field metric is not, by itself, a new theory direction.

### CG-NG-004 — Euclidean/Lorentzian separation

The frozen first RQIR-driven spectral inverse-kernel ansatz is Euclidean-safe but analytically develops an unavoidable below-threshold opposite-residue timelike pole for every `beta>0`. This validates the need for an independent Lorentzian consistency gate.

### CG-NG-005 — finite-order low-energy EFT degeneracy

A gapped positive continuum is analytic strictly below threshold and can be absorbed into local gravitational EFT Wilson coefficients at any fixed finite derivative order. Deep-IR detector optimization cannot identify that candidate against C5.

### CG-NG-006 — Gaussian mediator identity

A positive KL spin-2 continuum with linear conserved-stress coupling is exactly representable by an ordinary positive-norm continuum/tower of quantum spin-2 mediators. Matching retarded and Hadamard kernels gives the same Gaussian CTP influence functional, so no Fisher/resource improvement restores gravity-specific novelty.

### NG-FUNNEL-001 — nonlinearity is not enough

Known nonlocal gravity, asymptotic-safety vertex expansions, nonlinear massive gravity and stochastic classical–quantum gravity all contain higher-order structures. A nonzero three-point function or nonlinear vertex is therefore not by itself a distinguishing signature.

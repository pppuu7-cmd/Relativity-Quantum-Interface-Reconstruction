## Retained project results

### CG-NG-003 — reference degeneracy

Standard perturbative quantum GR cannot be promoted as a new model when C5 is the comparator.

### CG-NG-004 — Euclidean/Lorentzian separation

The first RQIR-driven inverse-kernel ansatz passes a Euclidean no-zero check but develops an unavoidable opposite-residue timelike pole after Lorentzian continuation.

### CG-NG-005 — finite-order low-energy EFT degeneracy

A gapped positive continuum is analytic strictly below threshold and is degenerate with local gravitational EFT Wilson directions at any fixed finite derivative order.

### CG-NG-006 — Gaussian mediator identity

A positive KL spin-2 continuum with linear conserved-stress coupling is exactly representable by an ordinary continuum/tower of positive-norm quantum spin-2 mediators at Gaussian level.

### NG-FUNNEL-001 — nonlinearity is not enough

Known quantum and classical-stochastic comparator classes already contain nonlinear/higher-point structure.

### NG-FUNNEL-002 — symmetric higher cumulant is not enough

A fully symmetrized non-Gaussian cumulant can be reproduced by a classical non-Gaussian random field.

### NG-FUNNEL-003 — Ward/soft locking is necessary but not sufficient

Diffeomorphism/soft identities reduce arbitrary mediator freedom but are also present in ordinary C5/covariant quantum-gravity comparator models.

### NG-FUNNEL-004 — soft lock is not novelty

For a candidate retaining the standard massless-GR/diffeomorphism boundary, leading and subleading soft-graviton relations are consistency locks. Subsubleading deviations are not unique because local EFT operators can contribute there.

### NG-FUNNEL-005 — class-envelope saturation

The first finite post-Gaussian protocol has 8 reduced coordinates after exact locks. Treating broad C3/C4/C5 class capabilities as independent coordinate directions saturates the full 8D space. This is not a physics no-go; it proves that theory-class labels cannot replace derivatives of fixed finite comparator models.

## Submission wording guardrail

Always distinguish:

- consistency rejection of a frozen model version;
- exact comparator identity;
- regime-specific non-identifiability;
- comparator-instantiation blocking;
- absence of a novelty certificate.

Do not translate the latter four categories into the statement that a broad existing theory is globally ruled out.

## Working section title

### Existing gravity frameworks as staged controls in the RQIR funnel

A central methodological point is that failure of an RQIR promotion gate is not synonymous with falsification of an entire gravity framework. The funnel distinguishes internal inconsistency, absence of a model-specific observable, exact degeneracy with a comparator, statistical non-identifiability, and lack of physical resource closure. Existing models can therefore serve as calibrated controls even when they cannot be promoted as the new Candidate Gravity proposed here.

Semiclassical mean gravity occupies the earliest control layer. Its representative equation couples a classical metric to the expectation value of the quantum stress tensor. Such a construction may be adequate in its controlled domain, but mean-source closure alone does not supply the independent gravitational fluctuation and ordered-response hierarchy required for a quantum-interface claim. RQIR therefore uses semiclassical gravity as a C1 baseline rather than treating agreement with a mean gravitational field as evidence for quantized gravity.

Stochastic gravity survives substantially deeper. The Einstein–Langevin and closed-time-path/influence-functional formulations derive stress-energy fluctuations, induced metric noise and dissipative response within one open-system description. Consequently, a Candidate Gravity model that differs from semiclassical gravity only by adding excess noise is not strongly identified: an admissible stochastic metric may reproduce the same lower-order observables. In the RQIR funnel, stochastic gravity is therefore a strong C2 comparator rather than an easy target for rejection.

Classical-channel and measurement-feedback gravity provide a complementary control. These models can reproduce an effective Newtonian interaction while necessarily coupling the interaction to decoherence/noise determined by the classical information channel. In the standard Kafri–Taylor–Milburn construction the channel cannot create entanglement between the interacting quantum systems. This identifies a qualitatively different late-stage discriminator: a signal must be tested not only against classical force and noise models but also against the complete set of classical-channel simulations allowed by the calibration and nuisance model.

Modern postquantum classical-gravity theories make this comparison more demanding. They couple quantum matter to a fundamentally classical but stochastic spacetime and can generate metric fluctuations, decoherence and observable strain/power spectra. Their constraint and Bianchi properties are realization-dependent, and current work includes explicitly modified consistency constructions. We therefore do not use an obsolete mean-field straw man as the strongest classical comparator; the RQIR funnel requires comparison against the best specified contemporary classical–quantum realization applicable to the observable under study.

Standard perturbative quantum general relativity provides a different type of negative control. In our repository it passes the initial low-energy state/dynamics/Newtonian gates but fails promotion as a new Candidate Gravity because it is exactly the C5 reference class. This is a novelty failure, not a consistency failure. It demonstrates that the mere presence of a quantized metric or graviton does not generate an independent theory direction once standard low-energy quantum GR is already included among the comparators.

Nonlocal and form-factor quantum-gravity models survive later still. Specific entire-form-factor constructions can avoid extra propagator poles and recent analyses provide positive spectral representations for important theory classes. Their difficulty in the RQIR funnel is therefore not necessarily basic consistency; it is operational distinctness. A fixed form factor must yield a finite observable that is not absorbed by ordinary gravitational EFT coefficients, source calibration, transfer-function uncertainty or another allowed comparator. This class is especially important for the present work because the first RQIR-driven spectral ansatz was Euclidean-safe but failed its Lorentzian pole gate, illustrating why no-extra-pole and spectral-positivity conditions must be tested before detector optimization.

Program-level approaches such as asymptotic safety are not assigned a binary pass/fail status until a concrete effective realization is specified. The RQIR contract acts on a defined state space, dynamics, retarded response, fluctuation hierarchy and detector-facing map. A broad program can therefore be scientifically promising while remaining operationally blocked at model instantiation for a particular laboratory discriminator.

The resulting staged comparison is itself part of the contribution. It prevents a common ambiguity in quantum-gravity phenomenology: a model may be internally consistent yet experimentally degenerate; experimentally distinct at the raw-response level yet eliminated by calibration; or statistically identifiable yet require unphysical resources. By recording the first gate at which each model class ceases to support a stronger claim, RQIR turns negative results into reusable constraints on subsequent theory construction.

## Claims that are safe for the final paper if later audits remain consistent

1. RQIR distinguishes **theory falsification** from **failure to support a stronger operational claim**.
2. Mean-only semiclassical gravity is an early comparator, while stochastic and postquantum classical gravity are stronger late comparators.
3. Standard perturbative quantum GR is a valid theory control but cannot constitute a novel candidate relative to C5.
4. Specific ghost-free nonlocal/form-factor models may pass early consistency gates and must therefore be treated as serious quantum comparators.
5. The rejection of `ANSATZ-RQIR-CTP-001` v0.1 demonstrates empirically within the project why Euclidean stability and Lorentzian spectral consistency must be separate gates.

## Wording to avoid

Do not write that RQIR has "disproved semiclassical gravity", "disproved stochastic gravity", "disproved postquantum classical gravity", "disproved nonlocal gravity", or "disproved asymptotic safety" unless a future calculation genuinely establishes such a theorem with the required scope. Use phrases such as `fails promotion under the specified RQIR claim`, `remains degenerate with comparator X`, `blocked pending a concrete observable map`, or `rejected for this frozen model version`.

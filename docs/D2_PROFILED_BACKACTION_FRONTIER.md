## 1. Why raw signal attenuation was not enough

Iterations 043–045 quantified the ordered-response attenuation caused by the minimum reciprocal-linear quantum backaction needed to acquire same-copy mean Fisher.

But RQIR's actual inference object is

`F_beta|theta`,

not detector signal norm alone.

Backaction can rotate both the hidden signal and the 22 source nuisance detector derivatives. Therefore the safe same-copy information limit must be recomputed after full profiling.

Iteration 046 propagates the exact Toy009 same-time two-force dephasing superoperator through the complete hard-constrained D2 detector Jacobian while granting the centered calibration likelihood itself unchanged. This is deliberately optimistic: a real shared trajectory would also alter calibration score vectors and introduce additional detector/backaction nuisance.

## 9. Experimental architecture implication

The preferred D2 architecture now separates more clearly into roles:

1. **science / best4 covariance copies:** weak readout only; preserve response and nuisance geometry;
2. **strong mean calibration:** independent or sacrificial preparations;
3. **small source-amplitude metrology:** optimized jointly with calibration exposure;
4. **timing/additive references:** independently certified controls;
5. **shared strong readout:** allowed only if a future detector explicitly evades RQIR-NG-021/022 through QND, backaction evasion, nonreciprocity or another demonstrated mechanism.

This is a more conservative but internally consistent experimental design than an all-in-one trajectory.

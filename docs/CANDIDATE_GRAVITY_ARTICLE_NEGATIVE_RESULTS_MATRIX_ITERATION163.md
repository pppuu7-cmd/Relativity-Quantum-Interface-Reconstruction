## Article-level interpretation

Iteration 163 removes the Iteration 157 dRGT `alpha3` residual from the set of promotable novelty signals. The earlier residual remains valid relative to the smaller comparator basis used then, but it does not survive the expanded fixed C5 quotient and therefore is not robust evidence of a unique Candidate Gravity direction.

The publishable negative result is stronger and cleaner: a finite six-coordinate post-Gaussian TT protocol can become fully saturated by physically admissible comparator directions. Once saturation occurs, additional model classes cannot be distinguished algebraically without enriching the observable protocol. This is not a theory identity and must not be presented as one.

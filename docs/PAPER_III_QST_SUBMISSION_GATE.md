## QST final-3% work package

The 97% -> 100% promotion requires all of the following to be closed:

1. **Concrete experimental architecture**
   - express the resource-budget result as an explicit experimental architecture rather than only an abstract feasibility envelope;
   - identify the measured channel, calibration operations, control variables, source/detector roles, and the operational sequence needed to connect the theory-level resource budget to a realizable experiment;
   - preserve the boundary between a proposed architecture and experimentally demonstrated feasibility.

2. **Resource-budget and assumption validation**
   - re-audit the final resource-budget algebra and numerical values against canonical results;
   - preserve the frozen final-significance convention and check that no historical/noncanonical budget pair is used in the submission manuscript;
   - expose assumptions, limiting regimes, nuisance/resource dependencies, and any sensitivity to apparatus parameters explicitly.

3. **Publication-quality figures and tables**
   - produce the final QST-facing resource-flow / experimental-architecture figure(s);
   - produce a compact table mapping theoretical requirement -> measurable quantity -> calibration requirement -> physical resource cost -> limiting assumption;
   - ensure every plotted numerical quantity has a canonical machine-readable source.

4. **Reviewer-style reproducibility closure**
   - bind every quantitative manuscript claim to canonical script/result paths;
   - rerun or independently check the submission-critical calculations where appropriate;
   - provide a clean reproduction manifest and fail closed on missing provenance rather than filling gaps from memory or prose.

5. **QST-specific claims and scope alignment**
   - edit title, abstract, introduction, discussion, and conclusion so the manuscript foregrounds quantum sensing/metrology/experimental architecture where justified;
   - distinguish theoretical resource requirements, projected feasibility, and demonstrated experimental capability;
   - remove or soften any wording that exceeds the evidence actually provided by the frozen calculations.

6. **Final submission package**
   - complete journal-facing manuscript formatting, references, figure captions, data/code availability and reproducibility statements, and submission metadata;
   - perform a final internal reviewer pass for physics consistency, notation, citations, numerical consistency, and claim discipline.

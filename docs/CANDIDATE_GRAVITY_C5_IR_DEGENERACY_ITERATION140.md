## Candidate continuum in Euclidean momentum

Suppressing the common tensor factor, the beta-dependent continuum transfer is

`Delta D_E(q^2) = (beta/M_*^2) C(x)`,

`x=q^2/M_*^2`,

`C(x)=int_1^infty ds rho_hat(s)/(s+x)`,

`rho_hat(s)=exp(1-s)`.

For `|x|<1` and `s>=1`,

`1/(s+x)=sum_{n=0}^infty (-x)^n/s^(n+1)`.

Therefore

`C(x)=sum_{n=0}^infty (-x)^n A_(n+1)`,

with moments

`A_k=int_1^infty ds rho_hat(s)/s^k`.

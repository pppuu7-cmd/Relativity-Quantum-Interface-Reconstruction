# RQIR Iteration 054 — Toy011 Centered Profiled Resource Audit

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Scope:** exact-nearest-neighbour Toy011 candidates from Iteration 053, compared with the practical Toy009/Iteration-011 baseline.  
**Status:** statistical/resource audit; no apparatus forecast and no new-physics claim.

## 1. Question

Iteration 053 established that a spatially local five-site nearest-neighbour source can retain

- exact finite NP3 rank `24/25`;
- positive hidden-pair states;
- equal selected mean/centered-noise observables;
- a nonzero ordered-response discriminator.

That was an existence result. It did **not** establish that the local source remains practical after finite-noise nuisance profiling and physical resource conversion.

Iteration 054 therefore applies the centered hard-constrained Fisher machinery to the two retained Toy011 candidates and explicitly separates:

1. normalized nuisance/calibration geometry;
2. absolute detector signal power before normalization;
3. source-preparation QFI and physically simpler energy/Ramsey metrology.

This separation is mandatory: normalizing the detector beta vector to unit norm can hide a large physical signal-rate penalty.

Relative to Toy009:

| source | D1 raw two-band Fisher proxy | D2 raw two-band Fisher proxy |
|---|---:|---:|

This is separate from calibration cost.

### D1

The corresponding normalized calibration-cost ratios are approximately

- Toy011 response: `68.93x` Toy009;
- Toy011 conditioning: `20.29x` Toy009.

Therefore the local response-oriented source has the larger raw response but a substantially worse nuisance-calibration burden. The conditioning-oriented source sacrifices additional raw signal but is much cheaper to calibrate.

### RQIR-RESOURCE-025 — locality creates a multi-resource tradeoff

> A locality-constrained source must not be ranked by raw detector signal or exact conditioning alone. In the current Toy011 manifold, locality introduces at least two independent penalties — absolute detector signal rate and normalized nuisance-calibration cost — which can move in opposite directions. The correct objective is total `F_beta|theta / T_wall`, not either quantity separately.

The numerical ratios above assume equal physical Fisher rate per **row-normalized** calibration coordinate. They are therefore geometry/resource diagnostics, not SI-hour predictions. Physical row transduction remains to be attached.

This is an important positive result: enforcing nearest-neighbour locality does not by itself make the hidden preparation coordinate intrinsically inaccessible.

## 8. Source-amplitude nuisance geometry is surprisingly stable

Using each source's own optimized centered D2 calibration weights, the independent source prior `C_alpha` required to restore 90% at calibration scale `lambda>1` is:

| `lambda` | Toy009 | Toy011 response | Toy011 conditioning |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1.05 | `192.60` | `208.02` | `212.36` |
| 1.10 | `100.80` | `108.52` | `110.75` |
| 1.20 | `54.91` | `58.77` | `59.94` |
| 1.50 | `27.37` | `28.91` | `29.44` |
| 2.00 | `18.18` | `18.96` | `19.25` |

At `lambda=1`, finite `C_alpha` cannot push the profile above exactly 90% because the calibration weights were themselves chosen at the known-amplitude 90% boundary.

The similarity of these curves shows that nearest-neighbour locality has **not** opened a new catastrophic beta/source degeneracy. The dominant present cost is instead the calibration Fisher required to control the other 22 source directions plus the lower absolute detector signal.

## 9. Scientific interpretation

Iteration 054 gives a cleaner answer than the raw Toy011 source scan:

1. **Positive:** the local source retains essentially the same fundamental source-amplitude QFI as Toy009.
2. **Positive:** the normalized `C_alpha(lambda)` nuisance structure remains similar; no new principle-level identifiability obstruction appears.
3. **Negative:** the currently sampled local candidates have substantially worse calibration geometry.
4. **Negative:** their absolute D1/D2 signal is substantially smaller.
5. **Design consequence:** the response-oriented Toy011 point is not necessarily the better physical experiment. The conditioning-oriented point is weaker in raw signal but wins strongly in calibration cost and Ramsey-metrology rate.

Toy011 therefore remains a **physicality-constrained research branch**, not the new RQIR baseline.

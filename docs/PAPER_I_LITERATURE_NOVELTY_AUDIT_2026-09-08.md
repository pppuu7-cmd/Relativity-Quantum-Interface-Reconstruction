## Executive result

The literature audit changes the *positioning* of Paper I but does not reopen its frozen scientific result.

The following ingredients are established background and must **not** be advertised as RQIR inventions:

1. separation between the stress-energy expectation value and stress-energy fluctuations;
2. the stress-energy noise kernel in stochastic gravity;
3. the Schwinger–Keldysh / closed-time-path (CTP) and influence-functional treatment of fluctuations and dissipation/response;
4. the fact that symmetric/noise and antisymmetric/dissipative response kernels are different pieces of the second-order open-system structure;
5. informational completeness/incompleteness of finite quantum measurements as a general tomography concept;
6. gravity-mediated entanglement and other information-theoretic witnesses of nonclassicality;
7. classical, stochastic, measurement-feedback and postquantum alternatives to standard quantized gravity.

The strongest defensible RQIR Paper-I contribution is therefore the **combination and operational organization** of these ingredients around a restricted inverse problem:

`declared finite source calibration -> physical equivalence class -> hidden null direction -> specified ordered-response functional -> detector-facing discriminant`,

while explicitly separating this source-side distinguishability from any claim that the gravitational mediator is quantum.

The finite theorem `RQIR-THM-001` should be presented as a compact proposition that makes this calibration-quotient logic explicit and physically admissible (positivity preserved), **not** as a claim that the underlying linear-algebra fact is unprecedented.

## 1. Established stochastic-gravity / CTP background

### Hu and Verdaguer — stochastic gravity

B. L. Hu and E. Verdaguer, *Stochastic Gravity: Theory and Applications*, Living Reviews in Relativity 11, 3 (2008), DOI: `10.12942/lrr-2008-3`.

Relevant established content:

- semiclassical gravity is sourced by `<T_ab>`;
- stochastic gravity introduces the symmetrized stress-energy noise kernel;
- the influence-functional / CTP effective action contains both fluctuation/noise and dissipation/response structure;
- fluctuation and dissipation can be related in controlled stationary/thermal settings.

**RQIR implication:** Paper I may use `J`, `N`, `D/chi^R` as an ordered operational hierarchy, but must not imply that discovering a noise/response split is new. The novelty target is the calibration quotient and reconstruction logic.

## 5. Full-QFT matter and the current entanglement-witness debate

### Aziz and Howl (2025)

J. Aziz and R. Howl, *Classical theories of gravity produce entanglement*, Nature 646, 813–817 (2025), DOI: `10.1038/s41586-025-09595-7`.

They argue that when matter is treated in full QFT, local classical-gravity theories can generate entanglement through quantum matter propagation, so the binary observation of entanglement is not by itself universally unambiguous across the enlarged comparator class.

### Tang et al. (preprint, 2025)

Z. Tang et al., *Matter-Mediated Entanglement in Classical Gravity: Suppression by Binding Potentials and Localization*, arXiv:`2512.13675`.

This preprint argues that the matter-mediated channel emphasized by Aziz–Howl is strongly suppressed in realistic bound/localized macroscopic systems. It is not yet treated here as a settled peer-reviewed resolution.

### Feng, Vedral and Marletto (2026)

T. Feng, V. Vedral and C. Marletto, *Collapse-based models for gravity do not violate the entanglement-based witness of nonclassicality*, Physical Review D 113, 104055 (2026).

This paper argues that particular collapse-based counterexamples violate the locality assumptions underlying the witness.

**RQIR implication:** Paper I must describe the witness debate neutrally. The safest conclusion is methodological: the interpretation of a measured witness depends on the admitted comparator class and its assumptions. That statement is stronger and more durable than selecting a winner in an active debate.

## 6. Beyond the Newtonian-potential regime

L.-Q. Chen and F. Giacomini, *Quantum Effects in Gravity Beyond the Newton Potential from a Delocalized Quantum Source*, Physical Review X 15, 031063 (2025), DOI: `10.1103/hl1c-t8z9`.

**RQIR implication:** Paper I should avoid implying that all relevant gravity–quantum tests reduce to Newtonian entanglement protocols. The operational hierarchy is intended to accommodate dynamical field/response observables as well.

## 7. Informational completeness and the nullspace theorem

Quantum state tomography provides a mature language for informationally complete and informationally incomplete measurement sets. A finite measurement/calibration map that does not span the relevant operator space generically leaves indistinguishable state directions.

Therefore the mathematical core of `RQIR-THM-001` should be positioned carefully:

- **not novel by itself:** finite linear measurements can have nontrivial kernels; informational completeness is established tomography theory;
- **RQIR-specific role:** the retained null direction is constrained to be a physical Hermitian traceless perturbation around an interior density operator, and a specified ordered-response functional is tested on that direction;
- **constructive value:** Toy009/Toy010 certify positivity, exact calibration equality, nonzero response and detector-facing survival simultaneously in the RQIR source-information setting;
- **design value:** the calibration-steering relation makes the nullspace geometry an experimental design variable rather than only a statement of incomplete tomography.

### Safe theorem wording

Preferred:

> We formulate a finite-dimensional calibration-nullspace proposition tailored to the RQIR source-response problem and give constructive positive-state realizations.

Avoid:

> We prove for the first time that incomplete measurements can leave indistinguishable quantum states.

# RQIR Toy Model 005 — Exact Newtonian Density-Channel Embedding

**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**Labels:** `DRV`, `NUM`, `OPEN`  
**Purpose:** replace the arbitrary positive source observable of Toy Model 004 by an exact weak-field mass-density / Newtonian-potential observable for a finite-mode quantum source.

## 1. Result in one line

A positive finite-dimensional operator `B` can be embedded exactly as the Newtonian potential produced by a one-particle mass-density operator at one fixed probe point.

For the explicit five-level RQIR witness, one can choose a **real symmetric** Hamiltonian/source construction such that

\[
\boxed{
\langle H\rangle_+=\langle H\rangle_-,
\quad
\langle \Phi_p(t)\rangle_+=\langle \Phi_p(t)\rangle_-\;\forall t,
\quad
N_{\Phi,+}(t,0)=N_{\Phi,-}(t,0)\;\forall t,
\quad
D_{\Phi,+}(t,0)\neq D_{\Phi,-}(t,0).
}
\]

This is a genuine physical embedding of **one Newtonian gravitational readout channel**, but it is **not yet** an equal-full-stress-energy or covariant GR null pair.

## 2. Physical-embedding lemma PE-1

Let `B` be any positive Hermitian operator on a `d`-dimensional one-particle Hilbert space. By the spectral theorem,

\[
B=V\,\operatorname{diag}(b_1,\ldots,b_d)V^\dagger,
\qquad b_a>0.
\]

Define localized one-particle modes

\[
|x_a\rangle=\sum_i V_{ia}|E_i\rangle,
\]

so that

\[
B=\sum_a b_a|x_a\rangle\langle x_a|.
\]

In a one-particle sector, define local number projectors

\[
\hat n_a=|x_a\rangle\langle x_a|,
\qquad \sum_a\hat n_a=I,
\]

and a pointlike weak-field mass density

\[
\hat\rho(\mathbf x)=m\sum_a\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf x-\mathbf x_a)\hat n_a.
\]

For a probe position `x_p`, the Newtonian potential operator generated by this density is

\[
\hat\Phi_p
=-Gm\sum_a\frac{\hat n_a}{r_a},
\qquad
r_a=|\mathbf x_p-\mathbf x_a|.
\]

Choose an arbitrary positive length scale `L` and place the modes so that

\[
\boxed{r_a=\frac{L}{b_a}}.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\hat\Phi_p=-\frac{Gm}{L}B.
}
\]

Therefore every positive finite-dimensional `B` has an exact representation as a **single-point Newtonian potential observable** of a one-particle finite-site mass distribution.

### What PE-1 does not say

It does **not** imply that an arbitrary `B` is a local covariant stress tensor, that the complete Hamiltonian is a relativistic local QFT, or that all gravitational probes see identical source histories. It establishes only a precise weak-field operational embedding for one smeared/readout channel.

## 3. Why this is physically standard in the Newtonian limit

For nonrelativistic quantized matter, the mass-density operator is of the form

\[
\hat\rho(\mathbf r)=m\hat\Psi^\dagger(\mathbf r)\hat\Psi(\mathbf r),
\]

or, in a first-quantized fixed-particle-number description,

\[
\hat\rho(\mathbf r)=m\sum_i\delta^{(3)}(\mathbf r-\hat{\mathbf r}_i).
\]

The Newtonian potential is the Green-function smearing of this density,

\[
\hat\Phi(\mathbf x)
=-G\int d^3x'\frac{\hat\rho(\mathbf x')}{|\mathbf x-\mathbf x'|}.
\]

Toy 005 is the discrete finite-mode restriction of exactly this structure.

Seed reference for this Newtonian operator structure: Howl et al., PRX Quantum 2, 010325 (2021), Appendix A.

## 5. Exact equalities enforced by the Fourier-span construction

For

\[
B(t)=e^{iHt}Be^{-iHt},
\]

the construction imposes, up to numerical roundoff,

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_+H)=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_-H)=3.2,}
\]

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Tr}[\rho_+B(t)]
=
\operatorname{Tr}[\rho_-B(t)]
\quad \forall t,

\]

and

\[
\boxed{
N_{B,+}(t,0)=N_{B,-}(t,0)

with centered symmetrized kernel

\[
N_B(t,0)=\frac12\langle\{\delta B(t),\delta B(0)\}\rangle.

\[
|\Delta\langle B\rangle|_{\max}<6\times10^{-15},

## 7. Explicit five-site Newtonian realization

Diagonalize `B` with a real orthogonal matrix `V` and use its eigenvectors as five localized site modes. In that site basis,

\[
B_{\rm site}=\operatorname{diag}(b_1,\ldots,b_5).
\]

Choose

\[
r_a=L/b_a.
\]

If the nearest site is normalized to distance `L0`, choose `L=5.53112 L0`. Then

\[
\boxed{
(r_1,r_2,r_3,r_4,r_5)/L_0
\approx
(5.53112,2.21089,1.44295,1.27948,1.00000).
}
\]

A source of mass `m` occupying exactly one of these coherent modes produces at the fixed probe

\[
\boxed{
\hat\Phi_p(t)=-\frac{Gm}{L}B(t).
}
\]

Consequently,

\[
\langle\Phi_p(t)\rangle_+=\langle\Phi_p(t)\rangle_-\quad\forall t,
\]

\[
N_{\Phi,+}(t,0)=N_{\Phi,-}(t,0)\quad\forall t,
\]

but

\[
D_{\Phi,+}(t,0)\neq D_{\Phi,-}(t,0),
\]

with the expected powers of `Gm/L` multiplying the kernels.

The corresponding site-basis Hamiltonian is real symmetric,

\[
H_{\rm site}\approx
\begin{pmatrix}
3.32831&1.31670&0.98110&0.61320&1.18889\\
1.31670&1.86853&0.10773&0.49490&1.05759\\
0.98110&0.10773&3.61765&0.18506&-0.25465\\
0.61320&0.49490&0.18506&3.28024&0.45425\\
1.18889&1.05759&-0.25465&0.45425&3.90527
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

This has the standard form of a finite tight-binding / coupled-mode Hamiltonian: diagonal onsite energies plus real coherent couplings.

## 8. Stronger interpretation now justified

Toy 004 showed algebraically

\[
(\langle H\rangle,\langle B\rangle,N_B)\not\Rightarrow D_B.
\]

Toy 005 strengthens this to an operational weak-gravity statement:

\[
\boxed{
(\langle H\rangle,\langle\Phi_p\rangle,N_{\Phi_p})
\not\Rightarrow
D_{\Phi_p}
}
\]

for a physically admissible finite-mode Newtonian mass-density source.

Thus a gravitational experiment calibrated only at the **mean-potential** and **symmetrized potential-noise** level can, in principle, leave an independent ordered/retarded source-response degree of freedom unconstrained.

This still does **not** imply quantized geometry: the response is a property of quantum matter, and stochastic/semiclassical influence-functional formalisms already contain matter-induced dissipation/response.

## 9. Critical remaining limitation: not a full-stress-energy null pair

In the site basis, the local populations of `rho_+` and `rho_-` are not identical. For the displayed witness their differences at `t=0` are approximately

\[
\Delta p_a
\approx
(0.00040,\;0.00476,\;-0.03890,\;0.04121,\;-0.00748).
\]

They cancel in the particular weighted potential `B`, but a second probe at another position can generally distinguish them already at the mean-field level.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\langle\Phi_p\rangle_+=\langle\Phi_p\rangle_-
\not\Rightarrow
\langle T_{\mu\nu}(x)\rangle_+=\langle T_{\mu\nu}(x)\rangle_-.
}
\]

This is the main open gate.

## 10. New hierarchy of null-pair strength

Toy 005 motivates explicit null-pair grades:

- **NP0:** equal global scalar (e.g. total mean energy) only;
- **NP1:** equal chosen gravitational readout mean;
- **NP2:** equal chosen readout mean + symmetrized noise;
- **NP3:** equal a finite multiprobe/multipole set of means + noises;
- **NP4:** equal complete relevant smeared stress-energy mean + noise in a declared spacetime domain;
- **NP5:** NP4 plus controlled apparatus/source stress-energy and relativistic conservation/gauge gates.

Toy 005 reaches **NP2** for one Newtonian potential channel while exhibiting a `D/chi^R` split.

The project should not call it a full gravity null pair until at least NP4/NP5 conditions are met.

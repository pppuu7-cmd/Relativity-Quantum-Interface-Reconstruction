Please consider my manuscript, “Relativity–Quantum Interface Reconstruction II: Statistical Identifiability, Nuisance Geometry, and Detectability in Detector-Facing Likelihoods,” as a Regular Article in Physical Review Research and as part of a joint submission with “Relativity–Quantum Interface Reconstruction I: Operational Hierarchy, Ordered Stress–Energy Information, and Finite Calibration-Nullspace Discriminants.”

Paper I establishes the exact calibration-aware source-side layer. Paper II asks the distinct statistical question of whether a detector-facing target direction survives nuisance profiling and how independent calibration information changes local precision. The separation between the two manuscripts is intentional: Paper I closes the exact source/calibration quotient, whereas Paper II closes the nuisance-profiled likelihood layer before any physical resource forecast is made.

The main Paper-II results are a nuisance-profiled projection/Schur geometry that remains valid for rank-deficient nuisance models; an exact variational formula with positive-semidefinite independent nuisance precision; nuisance-coordinate invariance and calibration-information monotonicity; an exposure no-go for exact score alignment; a shared-versus-branch-specific nuisance bound; and an exact two-band self-calibration law. The implementation is frozen by a seven-test deterministic reference certificate and independently stress-tested over 10,000 randomized problems including rank deficiency and correlated covariance. A minimal counterexample shows that threshold deletion of a weak but exactly collinear nuisance direction can manufacture a false unit-information signal.

The manuscript is deliberately conservative in interpretation. It does not claim new physics, global nonlinear identifiability, apparatus readiness, or that positive Fisher information implies practical detectability. Physical resource conversion is reserved for RQIR III.

All code and numerical data needed for the tables and figures are publicly available in the project repository, and the manuscript includes both a Data Availability Statement and a substantive-AI-use disclosure consistent with APS policy.

## Frozen v0.1 choices

- Model ID: `ANSATZ-RQIR-KL-002`.
- Massless spectral weight: exactly the reference GR pole weight.
- Continuum support: `mu^2>=M_*^2`.
- Continuum shape: `(beta/M_*^2) exp(1-mu^2/M_*^2)`.
- No isolated continuum pole.
- No independent tuning of static correction, absorptive response and vacuum Gaussian continuum noise.

## Explicit non-assumptions

The model does not assume that:

- positive two-point spectral measure proves nonlinear quantum gravity consistency;
- the spectral continuum is new in the literature;
- the continuum consists of literal fundamental Pauli–Fierz particles;
- a branch cut is itself evidence for quantum gravity;
- a raw beta-dependent signal survives C4/C5/C6 or detector nuisance profiling;
- the benchmark spectral shape is fundamental.

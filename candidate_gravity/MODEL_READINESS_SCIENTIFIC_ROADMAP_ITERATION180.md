## Current interpretation at Iteration 180

- Actual model readiness: `24%`.
- The next possible small increase is to `25%`, but only after the remaining comparator foundation is genuinely finite enough for a full quotient.
- The first major jump is possible when a residual survives that full quotient: comparator closure plus first residual milestone would move `24% -> 30%` if achieved together.
- Negative results can add `0%` while still materially improving the paper and preventing false novelty claims.

## Mandatory rule for future iterations

Every research log and recovery delta must state:

# RQIR Nonlinear Comparator Audit — Iteration 142

**Date:** 2026-08-31  
**Purpose:** determine what additional structure a post-Gaussian Candidate Gravity must contain after `CG-NG-006` proved that a positive linear-Gaussian spin-2 continuum is exactly reproducible by an ordinary quantum mediator continuum.

## Question exported by Iteration 141

What is the earliest higher-order object that is both:

1. derived from one gravity dynamics;
2. not fixed by the Gaussian two-point influence functional alone;
3. not generically reproducible by a classical stochastic metric;
4. not freely reproducible by an ordinary hidden/KK quantum mediator with arbitrary phenomenological self-interactions;
5. still distinct from standard C5 quantum GR and known nonlinear/nonlocal quantum-gravity models?

The first-wave answer is negative but constraining: **nonlinearity by itself is not sufficient**.

## A. Weakly nonlocal / entire-form-factor gravity

A fixed generally covariant nonlocal action does not merely modify the propagator. Expanding the same covariant action generates three- and higher-graviton vertices with momentum-dependent form factors. Existing weakly nonlocal constructions are explicitly designed so that selected entire/nonpolynomial form factors can avoid new propagator poles while improving ultraviolet behavior; nonlinear curvature terms and potentials are part of the same theory.

### RQIR funnel interpretation

- two-point pole/positivity: can PASS for specifically audited form factors;
- nonlinear vertices: PRESENT and derived from the same covariant action;
- Ward/diffeomorphism structure: inherited from the covariant action, subject to gauge/quantum consistency audits;
- RQIR higher-point map: feasible in principle but must be instantiated for a fixed form factor/action;
- novelty implication: **a generic momentum-dependent three-graviton vertex is not enough to distinguish a new model**, because this comparator already has one.

### Design lesson

A future ansatz must not claim novelty merely from `Gamma^(3) != Gamma_GR^(3)`. It needs a specific linked relation among two-point, three-point and matter-coupling observables that is not already an arbitrary nonlocal-form-factor freedom.

## B. Asymptotic safety

Functional-RG vertex expansions compute momentum-dependent graviton correlation functions beyond the propagator. Existing calculations include dynamical three- and four-graviton vertices, and recent work reconstructs quantum effective actions/equations of motion from multi-graviton correlation functions. Lorentzian spectral-function work also provides a positive massless-graviton peak plus continuum in concrete truncations.

### RQIR funnel interpretation

- program label alone: still not a finite RQIR model;
- concrete truncation with `Gamma^(2), Gamma^(3), Gamma^(4)`: passes the post-Gaussian model-instantiation requirement much better than a propagator-only ansatz;
- remaining blockers: gauge/relational observable map, Lorentzian continuation for the selected truncation, finite detector/source response, comparator subtraction and nuisance profiling.

### Design lesson

Higher-point gravitational vertices are already part of a serious existing QG program. Our next candidate must be tested against a frozen asymptotic-safety vertex set, not just against GR tree-level amplitudes.

## C. Nonlinear massive gravity / bimetric / KK-like spin-2 sectors

Ghost-free dRGT massive gravity shows that nonlinear spin-2 self-interactions can be highly constrained rather than arbitrary. The nonlinear potential is chosen so the Boulware–Deser mode is removed and five degrees of freedom propagate; the same nonlinear structure generates Vainshtein screening in appropriate regimes. KK/bimetric realizations can supply both massless and massive spin-2 sectors with correlated interactions.

### RQIR funnel interpretation

- linear `4/3` vDVZ-type tensor signature is not decisive;
- adding nonlinear spin-2 self-couplings is also not decisive;
- the real discriminator must include the **constraint/self-coupling relations** of the full model and compare them with the corresponding hidden/KK/dRGT family.

### Design lesson

A phenomenological cubic coupling constant added to `KL-002` would fail the purpose of RQIR: an allowed massive-spin-2 comparator can simply be given a matching nonlinear interaction. The coupling must be locked by a stronger symmetry/constraint relation.

## D. Postquantum classical gravity

Modern classical–quantum gravity is not restricted to Gaussian additive noise. Covariant/classical–quantum path-integral formulations produce stochastic spacetime dynamics, decoherence and diffusion from one action-like object. Explicit work studies generalized constraints and constraint algebra; renormalisation analyses relate the stochastic gravity action to quadratic-gravity structures; 2026 work derives stochastic tensor/scalar modes and effective matter actions.

### RQIR funnel interpretation

- non-Gaussian stochastic cumulants by themselves are not automatically quantum;
- a classical stochastic metric may have higher connected probability cumulants and nonlinear response;
- therefore `connected three-point function != 0` is not a sufficient quantum-gravity witness;
- the useful distinction must involve **operator ordering / nested commutator response / algebraic noncommutativity**, or another structure unavailable to a classical random field, while respecting the full classical–quantum comparator dynamics.

### Design lesson

The next candidate needs both a higher connected object and an ordered quantum-response object. RQIR must not identify a classical stochastic bispectrum as evidence for quantized geometry.

## E. Soft-graviton / diffeomorphism constraints

The leading soft-graviton theorem is universal, and in local EFT the subleading soft graviton theorem is especially robust; higher-dimension operators first modify softer orders under controlled assumptions. Soft relations can therefore serve as a **locking condition** tying nonlinear graviton/matter amplitudes to universal energy-momentum coupling rather than leaving every cubic vertex free.

This does not itself create novelty versus C5: standard quantum GR obeys the same leading universal soft structure. Its value is to reduce the C4 hidden-mediator nuisance family. A hidden mediator that reproduces the candidate must now reproduce both the finite higher-point response and the massless spin-2 soft/Ward identities.

## Result of Iteration 142

### NG-FUNNEL-001 — nonlinearity-alone degeneracy

**Statement:** The existence of nonlinear or non-Gaussian gravitational observables is not sufficient for RQIR promotion, because existing nonlocal quantum gravity, asymptotic-safety vertex expansions, ghost-free nonlinear massive gravity and stochastic classical–quantum gravity all contain nontrivial higher-order structures.

### NG-FUNNEL-002 — required ordered layer

**Statement:** A post-Gaussian RQIR candidate must separate at least:

- symmetrized connected higher cumulants;
- causal nonlinear response / nested commutator structures;
- symmetry/constraint relations tying those objects to the same universal gravitational coupling.

### NG-FUNNEL-003 — soft/Ward lock is necessary but not sufficient

A universal soft/Ward relation can eliminate generic unconstrained mediator fits, but it does not distinguish the candidate from standard C5 or other covariant quantum-gravity models. A residual finite-momentum/nonlocal/higher-point direction must still survive the C5/nonlocal/asymptotic-safety comparator space.

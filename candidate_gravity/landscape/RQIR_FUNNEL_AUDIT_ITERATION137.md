# RQIR Funnel Audit of Existing Gravity Model Classes — Iteration 137

**Date:** 2026-08-31  
**Purpose:** use existing gravity frameworks as controls, comparators, and design constraints for a new Candidate Gravity model. A funnel failure below means failure of a specified RQIR promotion criterion, **not** a general disproof of the underlying theory.

## Funnel used in this audit

The audit separates seven operational stages:

- **F0 — Dynamics:** one declared action/master equation/channel/path integral/CTP object.
- **F1 — Required limits:** Newtonian/GR plus ordinary QM/QFT limits appropriate to the model.
- **F2 — Consistency:** constraints/gauge, causality, positivity/unitarity/complete positivity, conservation/Bianchi/Ward structure.
- **F3 — RQIR hierarchy:** derive the applicable `J`, `N`, ordered/retarded response and parent influence/channel object from the same dynamics.
- **F4 — Comparator distinction:** identify a finite observable direction not exactly degenerate with a weaker comparator.
- **F5 — Hard-constraint discriminator:** survive the exact calibration quotient used by RQIR Paper I.
- **F6 — Statistical identifiability:** nonzero profiled `F_beta|theta` after nuisance/comparator directions.
- **F7 — Physical resources:** finite apparatus/resource certificate under RQIR Paper III.

No model is called experimentally discriminating before F5–F7. No model is called a novel Candidate Gravity merely for passing F0–F3.

## 1. Semiclassical mean gravity — C1

Representative structure:

`G_mn = 8 pi G <T_mn>`.

The quantum matter state enters through the expectation value of the stress tensor while the spacetime metric remains classical. This is a valid controlled comparator in appropriate regimes, but it does not by itself provide an independent quantum gravitational operator/noise hierarchy.

### RQIR classification

- F0: PASS as a declared hybrid/mean-field equation in its domain.
- F1: PASS/PARTIAL depending on the chosen semiclassical EFT implementation.
- F2: controlled renormalized semiclassical formulations exist, with the usual domain limitations.
- F3: **INSUFFICIENT FOR FULL QG PROMOTION** if the claimed discriminator requires gravitational `N`, noncommuting ordered response, or higher quantum gravitational correlators not reducible to matter expectation values.
- F4+: retain as C1 baseline rather than call it a failed theory.

### Article-useful statement

RQIR does not reject semiclassical gravity because the mean equation is mathematically meaningless; it rejects **mean-only closure as sufficient evidence for a quantum gravitational interface**.

## 2. Stochastic gravity — C2

Stochastic gravity extends semiclassical gravity by adding the stress-energy noise kernel and an Einstein–Langevin description. The CTP/Feynman–Vernon formulation naturally exposes fluctuation and dissipation structures.

### RQIR classification

- F0: PASS in its controlled open-system formulation.
- F1: PASS/PARTIAL by regime.
- F2: PASS/PARTIAL in the controlled linearized/large-N/open-system domains.
- F3: **STRONG PASS AS AN RQIR COMPARATOR** because mean, noise, and dissipative response are linked by a parent influence functional.
- F4: BLOCKED until a proposed Candidate Gravity supplies an observable that cannot be reproduced by an admissible classical stochastic metric/kernel.

### Consequence for our model

A model that only predicts extra gravitational noise is weak: C2 may absorb it. A stronger target is a linked signature involving ordered response/operator noncommutativity/higher statistics that a positive classical stochastic process cannot reproduce after the same calibration quotient.

## 3. Classical-channel / measurement-feedback gravity — C3a

Kafri–Taylor–Milburn-type constructions mediate the gravitational interaction through a classical measurement/feedback channel. In the standard model, reproducing the Newtonian interaction is accompanied by decoherence, and the classical channel cannot create entanglement between the two systems.

### RQIR classification

- F0: PASS — explicit open-system/channel dynamics.
- F1: PASS for the targeted Newtonian weak-interaction regime.
- F2: PASS at the CP master-equation level for the declared construction; dissipative variants address secular heating behavior.
- F3: PASS/PARTIAL — transfer and compulsory channel noise/decoherence are linked.
- F4/F5: a discriminator that certifies a non-LOCC entangling gravitational channel can distinguish this class; response/noise alone may not.

### Consequence for our model

RQIR must always ask whether a proposed signal is reproducible by measurement plus feedback. If yes, it is not yet evidence for a quantum gravitational mediator.

## 4. Postquantum classical gravity — C3b

Modern postquantum classical-gravity programs couple quantum matter to a fundamentally classical stochastic spacetime. Recent work derives stochastic tensor/scalar modes and observable power spectra; current literature also studies Bianchi-consistent variants and direct experimental tests.

### RQIR classification

- F0: PASS for a specified realization, not merely for the program label.
- F1: classical-GR limit is part of the construction.
- F2: **VERSION-DEPENDENT**. Constraint closure/Bianchi compatibility must be audited for the exact realization. Earlier analyses found extra-constraint/closure issues in broad classes, while newer variants explicitly target these consistency conditions.
- F3: PASS/PARTIAL for specified stochastic modes and matter coupling.
- F4/F5: remains a serious late comparator because it can predict spacetime noise, decoherence and classical gravitational-wave-like stochastic modes without quantizing the metric.

### Consequence for our model

A viable Candidate Gravity must beat the strongest current C3b realization, not an obsolete straw-man semiclassical equation.

## 5. Perturbative quantum GR EFT — C5

This is the repository's `ANSATZ-PQG-EFT-001` reference.

### RQIR classification

- F0–F3: PASS in the controlled weak-field low-energy EFT domain, subject to the explicit gauge/renormalization conventions already tracked by the reference branch.
- F4 novelty: **FAIL AS A NEW MODEL** because it is exactly comparator C5 by construction.

Retained negative result:

`CG-NG-003` — quantizing the weak-field metric within standard perturbative quantum-GR EFT does not create a model-specific novelty direction relative to C5.

This is a novelty failure, not a consistency failure.

## 6. Ghost-free nonlocal / form-factor gravity

Specific nonlocal gravity theories use entire or otherwise controlled form factors to modify the propagator while avoiding additional poles. Recent work derives positive spectral/Källén–Lehmann-type representations for important classes; other recent work studies explicit ghost-free conditions on curved backgrounds.

### RQIR classification

- F0: PASS for a **fixed action and fixed form factor**.
- F1: many constructions possess a smooth GR/local limit.
- F2: can PASS for specifically audited form factors; this must never be inferred just from the word `nonlocal`.
- F3: BLOCKED/PARTIAL until the model supplies the exact RQIR-facing CTP/noise/ordered-response hierarchy in one convention.
- F4: high-value gate. At low momentum, a Taylor expansion may be degenerate with ordinary higher-dimension gravitational EFT operators; a genuine distinction must use the resolved full form factor/nonanalytic structure.
- F5+: only after exact nuisance/calibration quotient.

### Design lesson from our rejected v0.1

Our Iteration-136 rejection shows why copying only a positive spectral density is insufficient. A safe new ansatz should prefer structural no-extra-pole mechanisms (for example an exponential of an entire function in a fixed model) rather than a multiplicative factor that is Euclidean-positive but forced through zero under Lorentzian continuation.

This is a **design prior**, not a decision to adopt an existing nonlocal model as our final theory.

## 7. Asymptotic safety

Asymptotic safety is a quantum-gravity program based on a nontrivial ultraviolet fixed point/quantum scale symmetry. Current reviews emphasize increasingly realistic matter content, Lorentzian progress and phenomenological pathways.

### RQIR classification

RQIR cannot test the program name directly. It needs a concrete effective trajectory/truncation that outputs:

- physical state/observable definition;
- Lorentzian retarded response;
- spectral/noise hierarchy if applicable;
- finite low-energy source-to-detector map.

Thus the program is not marked `FAIL`; it is **BLOCKED AT MODEL INSTANTIATION** until one specific effective realization is frozen. A sufficiently concrete asymptotic-safety form-factor model can then enter F0–F7 like any other candidate.

## Article-level conclusion from the first landscape audit

The funnel separates three scientifically different outcomes that should not be conflated:

1. **Consistency failure** — e.g. our `ANSATZ-RQIR-CTP-001` v0.1 at QG-004.
2. **Comparator/novelty failure** — e.g. standard perturbative QG is viable physics but cannot be promoted as a new model relative to C5.
3. **Operational incompleteness/blocking** — e.g. a broad quantum-gravity program without a frozen RQIR-facing observable kernel cannot yet be assigned a finite discriminator.

This classification itself is suitable for the future Candidate Gravity article because it makes negative results reproducible and prevents overclaiming.

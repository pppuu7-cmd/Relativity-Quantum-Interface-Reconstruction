# Candidate Gravity — Negative and Boundary Results

This file records scientifically useful failures, blockers and scope boundaries that must not be silently erased when the Candidate Gravity article funnel is prepared. A negative entry is not automatically a failure of Candidate Gravity; each entry carries its typed interpretation.

## Iteration 325 — incomplete cubic-topology validation gate

**Classification:** `FAIL_SCOPED_GATE_DESIGN_INCOMPLETE_CUBIC_TOPOLOGY__TESTED_SHIFTED_HN_INSERTIONS_PASS`

The arbitrary-incoming-momentum numerator validator originally enumerated only the six ordered pair and six ordered triple partitions of cubic target `(1,1,1)`. Consequently it tested 18 distinct shifted ghost/graviton H/N insertion requests, all of which passed the same-parent exact-geometry oracle by large numerical margins, but it could not test the singleton cubic `Tr(G0 K3)` insertion with multiindex `(1,1,1)`.

This is a **validation-gate completeness failure**, not a Candidate Gravity consistency failure and not a physical H/N-kernel failure. It is retained as article-quality methodological evidence for the rule that a green or red automated gate has no scientific interpretation until topology coverage itself is audited.

The defect was not repaired by weakening a threshold or editing the failed gate. Iteration 326 was created as a new gate version and added only the missing singleton topology while preserving the physical kernels, finite-difference step and frozen thresholds. Iteration 326 then passed the full `1 + 6 + 6` cubic topology on its historical H/N fixtures.

**Scoped article claim:** automated validation can fail for topology-coverage reasons even when all instantiated physical contractions pass; therefore coverage certificates must be separated from dynamical consistency certificates.

**Do not claim:** that Iteration 325 is evidence against Candidate Gravity, evidence for novelty, or evidence for a comparator residual.

## Iteration 327 — full topology but incompatible determinant background fixtures

**Classification:** `FAIL_SCOPED_GATE_DESIGN_ITERATION326_NOT_COMMON_CLOSED_TRIAD_BACKGROUND`

A higher-level authority audit found that Iteration 326 corrects the missing singleton and rebinds the incoming loop momentum `p`, but it loads graviton insertions from Iteration 319 and ghost insertions from Iteration 317 without rebinding their external Fourier momenta `qs` or metric perturbations `hs` to the common closed background used by Iterations 322/324.

The mismatch is explicit. The Iteration-319 graviton fixture differs from the closed triad in its third momentum with maximum component difference `0.36`; the Iteration-317 ghost fixture differs with maximum component difference `0.34`. Moreover the two files generate independent background perturbations (seed/scale `319/0.12` versus `317/0.2`). Therefore their numerators cannot be combined with one another and with Iteration-324 closed-triad denominators as one physical graviton-minus-ghost determinant trace.

Iteration 326 is retained, not rewritten. Its valid scope is narrowed to full-topology arbitrary-incoming-`p` validation on the respective historical H and N fixtures. The stronger common-background closed-triad interpretation is blocked pending a new gate version.

This is **not** a Candidate Gravity consistency FAIL and not a physical H/N-kernel FAIL. It is a validation-contract failure discovered before integration. It provides methodological evidence for the stronger rule that topology coverage, loop routing and same-background parent authority must all be certified independently before a functional-trace result is promoted.

A structural denominator census remains permissible: the 13 cubic sequences reduce under proved loop translations to one singleton, three pair/bubble and two triple/triangle families. This is not a nonzero-discontinuity certificate.

**Scoped article claim:** a full routed topology can still be scientifically non-combinable when numerator sectors were validated on different external/background fixtures; common-parent background authority is a separate gate.

**Do not claim:** that Iteration 327 refutes Candidate Gravity, establishes a determinant cut, establishes novelty, or changes the frozen physical H/N dynamics.

## Standing negative-result taxonomy

- `consistency FAIL`: a frozen physical consistency gate fails under its declared parent dynamics and parameter convention.
- `exact comparator identity`: the candidate coordinate is exactly reproduced by a comparator under the frozen quotient.
- `regime-specific non-identifiability`: the observable cannot identify the candidate in the stated regime, without implying global identity.
- `operational BLOCKED/FAIL`: execution, provenance, missing authority, or observability prevents a scientific conclusion.
- `near-degeneracy`: nonzero residual exists but is numerically/operationally close to a comparator direction.
- `absence of novelty certificate`: no robust comparator-surviving residual has yet been established.

As of Iteration 327, Candidate Gravity remains in the last category with active typed blockers; no robust comparator-subtracted residual exists.

## Linear source vertex and exact Ward identity

Stripping the common gravitational coupling, the one-graviton scalar vertex is

\[
V^{\mu\nu}(p,p')=
 p^\mu p'^\nu+p^\nu p'^\mu
-\eta^{\mu\nu}(p\!\cdot\!p'-m^2),
\qquad k=p'-p.
\]

It obeys

\[
k_\mu V^{\mu\nu}
=(p'^2-m^2)p^\nu-(p^2-m^2)p'^\nu.
\]

Thus the same scalar inverse propagator that defines the matter dynamics also controls the off-shell Ward identity. For on-shell scalar legs the physical source is transverse automatically.

# Candidate Gravity — Frozen Architectural Principles

**Status:** FROZEN DESIGN DOCTRINE  
**Date:** 2026-09-06  
**Scope:** Candidate Gravity construction under the RQIR program  
**Scientific effect:** architectural constraint only; **NON_PROMOTING**. This document does not change any physical, numerical, residual, ansatz, or readiness authority by itself.

## KG-AP-001 — Established core + irreducible novel sector

Candidate Gravity (KG) is **not** to be constructed as “new physics only”. Its target architecture is

\[
\mathcal M_{\rm KG}=\mathcal M_{\rm established}\oplus\Delta\mathcal M_{\rm novel},
\]

where:

1. `M_established` is the minimal structure required to reproduce physics that survives the applicable frozen RQIR gates and is needed for the relevant regime;
2. `Delta M_novel` is the minimal residual structure that cannot be represented by, absorbed into, or identified with the admitted comparator/equivalence classes;
3. no known structure is copied merely because it appears in another model: RQIR retains the **minimal representative of the surviving physical content**, not a union of model formalisms;
4. novelty is credited **only** to the irreducible comparator-subtracted sector, never to the established core.

Equivalent observable-level statement:

\[
\Delta\mathcal O_{\rm KG}
=\mathcal O_{\rm KG}-\Pi_{\rm known}\mathcal O_{\rm KG}.
\]

If `Delta O_KG = 0` within the frozen quotient/comparator definition, the construction may remain consistent but receives **NOVELTY FAIL / comparator identity** for that claimed effect. It is not promoted as new physics.

## KG-AP-002 — RQIR-guided construction is allowed; final-answer fitting is forbidden

KG may learn from existing models and prior RQIR outcomes in order to avoid already established failure modes and to inherit necessary viable structures. In particular, construction may use known lessons about:

- conservation / Ward consistency;
- analyticity and causal structure;
- pole, ghost, positivity and support restrictions;
- correct IR / GR limits where applicable;
- numerical robustness and identifiability;
- known comparator degeneracies and quotient identities.

However, this learning may constrain the **admissible model space** only. It must not force the final novelty result.

Frozen distinction:

\[
\text{learn from known gates}\neq\text{fit to the final answer}.
\]

A gate whose outcome is used directly to design or tune the same claimed novel signal cannot simultaneously serve as an independent confirmation of that signal.

## KG-AP-003 — Training / validation / holdout separation

For model-construction logic, RQIR gates should be classified where feasible as:

- **TRAINING / DESIGN gates:** known constraints and lessons that may shape KG;
- **VALIDATION gates:** frozen checks that KG is expected to satisfy after construction;
- **HOLDOUT gates:** independent checks not used to tune the claimed novel residual.

The scientific novelty claim requires success beyond the design information. Holdout predictions or holdout consistency tests must remain genuinely independent of the tuning path used to obtain the candidate.

## KG-AP-004 — Minimal novelty principle

Do not add a new field, operator, coupling, pole structure, nonlocal kernel, parameter, or other dynamical ingredient if the same claimed observable effect is already represented by an admitted comparator class under the frozen RQIR quotient.

Prefer the smallest irreducible extension compatible with the surviving established core.

Conceptually:

\[
\mathcal S_{\rm novel}
=\mathcal S_{\rm consistent}
\setminus
\bigcup_a \mathcal S_{{\rm comparator},a},
\]

where this expression is a design principle, not permission to treat incompletely mapped comparator space as exhausted.

## KG-AP-005 — No guaranteed passage of the full funnel

KG should be deliberately constructed to satisfy already-known necessary consistency conditions, but **full RQIR passage is not guaranteed by design**.

In particular, none of the following may be assumed in advance:

- existence of a nonzero robust comparator-subtracted residual;
- physical uniqueness of that residual;
- identifiability of new parameters;
- successful out-of-sample / holdout prediction;
- experimental accessibility;
- correctness of KG as a theory of nature.

A negative result at one of these independent stages must be retained as a legitimate scientific outcome.

## KG-AP-006 — Existing models are learning material and comparators, not ingredients to concatenate

RQIR may extract structural motifs from GR, EFT-like, higher-curvature, scalar-tensor, Lorentz-breaking, nonlocal, asymptotic, quantum-gravity-inspired, or other admissible families. KG must not become a mechanical sum of those models.

When multiple formulations occupy the same relevant comparator/equivalence class, KG inherits at most the minimal physical content needed for the established sector.

## KG-AP-007 — Required interpretation of a successful final KG

If KG eventually succeeds, the preferred interpretation is:

> established physics survives as a limiting/core sector, while RQIR isolates a minimal irreducible additional sector that survives comparator subtraction and independent validation.

Therefore the scientific novelty of KG is measured by `Delta M_novel` / `Delta O_KG`, not by the total size or complexity of the full model.

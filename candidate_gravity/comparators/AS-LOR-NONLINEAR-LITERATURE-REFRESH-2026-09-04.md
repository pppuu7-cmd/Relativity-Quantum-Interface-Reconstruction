### Pawlowski, Reichert & Wessely — arXiv:2507.22169 (2025)

*Self-consistent graviton spectral function in Lorentzian quantum gravity.*

This materially advances the Lorentzian **two-point** sector: a self-consistent full graviton spectral function is fed back into the spectral RG diagrams in a physical mass-shell renormalisation scheme. ### Pastor-Gutiérrez, Pawlowski, Reichert & Ruisi — arXiv:2412.13800v2 / PRD 111, 106005 (2025)

*$e^+e^-\to\mu^+\mu^-$ in the Asymptotically Safe Standard Model.*

The scattering amplitude uses momentum-dependent timelike 1PI correlation functions and reconstruction of graviton spectral functions. This is genuine progress toward Lorentzian observable amplitudes, but its physical object is a **matter scattering process**, not the same-parent pure-gravity nonlinear retarded three-response required by the RQIR target.

### Knorr — arXiv:2602.21285v2 (2026)

*Asymptotically (un)safe scattering amplitudes from scratch: a deep dive into the IR jungle.*

This computes leading quantum-gravity contributions to a scalar scattering amplitude and emphasizes that fixed-point information or standard RG improvement alone is insufficient: full momentum dependence can materially change amplitudes and purely gravitational couplings remain delicate. This strengthens, rather than relaxes, the RQIR prohibition on synthesising the missing nonlinear comparator from low-order running couplings or derivative-expansion data.

It is not a source-completed pure `hhh` retarded response.

### Chiesa, Pawlowski & Reichert — arXiv:2603.10168 (2026)

*Towards Two-to-Two Scattering of Scalars in Asymptotically Safe Quantum Gravity.*

This computes full momentum dependence of the **scalar–scalar–graviton** three-point scattering vertex for spacelike momenta, discusses analytic continuation to the Minkowski branch, reconstructs the timelike vertex and uses it in a graviton-mediated scalar scattering amplitude.

This is the closest new result to an operational Lorentzian three-point construction, but the vertex is `Gamma^{phi phi h}`, not the pure gravitational `Gamma^{hhh}` / source-completed nonlinear gravitational response required by the RQIR linked target. It therefore cannot be silently substituted into the comparator funnel.

### Assant, Litim & Reichert — arXiv:2606.19321 (2026)

*Spectral Functions of Lorentzian Quantum Gravity.*

This newer work computes Lorentzian spectral functions for graviton modes and the quantum effective action to **quadratic order in curvature**, extracting graviton-induced form factors. This materially strengthens the Lorentzian two-point/nonlocal-form-factor foundation.

Quadratic-curvature form factors and spectral propagators still do not furnish the missing source-completed pure-gravity three-response.

# RQIR Research Log — Iteration 001

**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**Phase:** bootstrap / operational reconstruction

## Objective

Initialize a model-independent research programme for the relativity–quantum interface and produce the first concrete discriminator construction.

## Completed

### ✅ Project charter established

The repository now explicitly rejects choosing a preferred quantum-gravity theory at initialization. Classical, semiclassical, stochastic, quantized, hybrid and emergent interfaces are treated as competing classes.

### ✅ Operational residual framework defined

For each observable,

\[
\Delta_A^{(B)}=O_A^{\rm obs}-O_A^{(B)},
\]

with mandatory baseline/regime/uncertainty/nuisance bookkeeping.

### ✅ Interface hierarchy defined

The programme tracks means, fluctuations, correlations, coherence, entanglement, causal structure and higher cumulants rather than identifying the interface solely with \(\langle T_{\mu\nu}\rangle\).

### ✅ Q1–Q7 channel atlas initialized

The first seven channels cover clocks, superposed sources, backreaction, gravity-mediated quantum information, geometry fluctuations, causal/process structure and low-energy QG EFT.

### ✅ Degeneracy principle strengthened using 2025 literature

Aziz & Howl (Nature 2025) demonstrate that classical gravity combined with full QFT matter can generate entanglement. Therefore a future observed entanglement witness cannot by itself be used by RQIR as an unambiguous proof of a quantized gravitational mediator.

Consequence: Q4 must use scaling/multi-observable fingerprints.

### ✅ First analytic toy model derived

Two source states,

\[
|A\rangle=(|2,0\rangle+|0,2\rangle)/\sqrt2,
\qquad
|B\rangle=|1,1\rangle,
\]

have identical mean mass at two localized modes but different mass covariance.

The weak-field source-statistics potential variance satisfies

\[
\operatorname{Var}_A(\Phi)=G^2m^2(1/r_L-1/r_R)^2,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}_B(\Phi)=0,
\]

while the mean potential is identical.

This yields the first concrete Q2–Q3–Q5 discriminator: **mean-field equality does not imply fluctuation-level equality**.

### ✅ Negative/limiting result recorded

The construction does not isolate quantum coherence because the NOON pure state and its incoherent two-branch mixture share the same diagonal occupation statistics in the zero-overlap approximation.

This classical-statistical degeneracy is now an explicit constraint on future RQIR tests.

## Current strongest conceptual result

The interface should not initially be parameterized only by “is the metric quantum?”. A more robust inverse problem is to determine which layers of quantum source information are transferred into operational gravitational observables:

\[
\langle T\rangle
\rightarrow C^{(2)}
\rightarrow C^{(3)},\ldots
\rightarrow \text{coherence/entanglement/process information}.
\]

The transfer law itself is the object to reconstruct.

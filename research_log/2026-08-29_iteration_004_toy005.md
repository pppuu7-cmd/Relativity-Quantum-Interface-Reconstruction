## Completed

### ✅ Analytic physical-embedding lemma PE-1

For any positive finite-dimensional Hermitian operator

\[
B=V\operatorname{diag}(b_a)V^\dagger,
\qquad b_a>0,
\]

define localized one-particle modes by the eigenvectors of `B` and place them at distances

\[
r_a=L/b_a
\]

from a fixed probe point. In the one-particle sector the Newtonian mass-density operator gives

\[
\hat\Phi_p=-Gm\sum_a\frac{\hat n_a}{r_a}
=-\frac{Gm}{L}B.
\]

Therefore an arbitrary positive `B` is not merely an abstract algebraic observable: it can be realized exactly as one **single-point Newtonian potential channel**.

This is a channel-specific embedding, not a proof that `B` is a covariant local stress tensor.

### ✅ Cleaner real-symmetric five-level witness found

A deterministic-seed real-symmetric search produced

\[
H=\operatorname{diag}(1,2,3,4,6),
\]

with a positive real-symmetric `B` and two positive density matrices

\[
\rho_\pm=I/5\pm0.08\Delta.
\]

The construction satisfies, to numerical roundoff,

\[
\langle H\rangle_+=\langle H\rangle_-=3.2,
\]

\[
\langle B(t)\rangle_+=\langle B(t)\rangle_-\quad\forall t,
\]

\[
N_{B,+}(t,0)=N_{B,-}(t,0)\quad\forall t,
\]

while the commutator/response differs.

Near

\[
t_*\approx3.58393
\]

we find

\[
N_+=N_-\approx0.288837,
\]

\[
D_+\approx+0.114476,
\qquad
D_-\approx-0.114476.
\]

Dense checks over one `2*pi` period gave maximum equality residuals below about `6e-15` for the mean and `3e-15` for the symmetrized kernel.

### ✅ Exact Newtonian five-site interpretation

The positive eigenvalues of `B` are

\[
(1.00000,2.50176,3.83320,4.32295,5.53112).
\]

Choosing the nearest site as one length unit gives relative probe distances

\[
(5.53112,2.21089,1.44295,1.27948,1.00000).
\]

In the site basis, the Hamiltonian is real symmetric and therefore has the form of a finite coupled-mode/tight-binding Hamiltonian with real onsite energies and coherent couplings.

Thus Toy 005 reaches a genuine weak-field mass-density realization of the ordered-kernel split.

### ✅ New null-pair strength grading introduced

- `NP0`: equal global scalar only;
- `NP1`: equal chosen gravitational readout mean;
- `NP2`: equal chosen readout mean + symmetrized noise;
- `NP3`: equal finite independent multiprobe/multipole mean/noise set;
- `NP4`: equal complete relevant smeared stress-energy mean/noise over declared domain;
- `NP5`: NP4 plus full source/apparatus conservation and relativistic/gauge controls.

Toy 005 is currently **NP2**, not NP4/NP5.

## Critical negative/limiting result

The two states do not have identical local site populations. At `t=0`, the local population differences are approximately

\[
(0.00040,0.00476,-0.03890,0.04121,-0.00748).
\]

These differences cancel in the chosen weighted potential, but a second independent probe can generally distinguish the preparations at mean-field level.

Therefore the project must not claim that Toy 005 matches the full stress-energy tensor.

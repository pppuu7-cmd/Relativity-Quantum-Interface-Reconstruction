## Iteration 002 — analytic no-go and hierarchy upgrade

### ✅ RQIR-NG-001: static density phase blindness

Compared

\[
|\psi_\pm\rangle=(|2,0\rangle\pm|0,2\rangle)/\sqrt2
\]

and the corresponding incoherent mixture.

For any operator diagonal in the joint occupation basis,

\[
F=F(n_L,n_R),
\]

all three preparations give the same expectation value and the same complete diagonal probability distribution.

For a controlled density-diagonal source–probe interaction

\[
U=\sum_\alpha|\alpha\rangle\langle\alpha|\otimes U_\alpha,
\]

the reduced probe state depends only on the source diagonal probabilities. Therefore no probe-only measurement can reveal the source relative phase in this idealized non-overlap model.

**Consequence:** a static superposition-versus-mixture comparison is not automatically a gravitational coherence witness.

### ✅ Ordered-kernel hierarchy introduced

The old single `C^(2)` source descriptor was replaced by

\[
\langle T\rangle,\qquad
N=\tfrac12\langle\{\delta T,\delta T\}\rangle,\qquad
D=\tfrac1{2i}\langle[\delta T,\delta T]\rangle,
\]

or equivalently a retarded response `chi^R`.

A Schwinger–Keldysh / closed-time-path generating functional was adopted as the formal parent object for all ordered correlators.

## Iteration 003 — finite-dimensional balanced search

### Search formulation

For `rho±=I/d ± eps Delta`, imposed

\[
Tr\Delta=0,
\quad
Tr(\Delta H)=0,
\quad
Tr[\Delta B(t)]=0,
\quad
Tr[\Delta\{B(t),B(0)\}/2]=0
\]

for all times represented exactly through the finite Fourier components generated by integer energy gaps.

Then searched for a nonzero component of

\[
Tr\left[\Delta\,[B(t),B(0)]/(2i)\right].
\]

### ✅ First balanced five-level witness found

A `d=5` example was found with positive `H`, positive `B`, and positive density matrices `rho±` such that

\[
\boxed{
\langle H\rangle_+=\langle H\rangle_-,
\quad
\langle B(t)\rangle_+=\langle B(t)\rangle_-\;\forall t,
\quad
N_+(t,0)=N_-(t,0)\;\forall t,
\quad
D_+(t,0)\neq D_-(t,0).
}
\]

Numerically the common mean energy is `3.4` in chosen units. At one checked time near `t=3.26726`,

\[
N_+=N_-\approx0.803678,
\]

while

\[
D_+\approx+0.535468,
\qquad
D_-\approx-0.535468.
\]

File: `docs/TOY_MODEL_004_BALANCED_FIVE_LEVEL_ORDERED_KERNEL.md`.

Reproducibility code: `analysis/search_balanced_ordered_kernel.py`.

### Numerical dimension observation

Random search performed in this iteration found no witness in:

- `d=2`: 500 trials;
- `d=3`: 1500 trials;
- `d=4`: 5000 trials;

while `d=5` produced a witness quickly for the recorded seed.

This is **not** proof that five levels are minimal. The lower-dimensional operator constraints were full rank for the sampled generic cases, but structured/degenerate counterexamples have not been excluded.

Working conjecture: `CONJ-RQIR-001` — a generic independent ordered-response direction under the chosen equality constraints may require dimension at least five.

## Critical limitation

Toy Model 004 has not yet embedded `B` into a covariantly conserved local or smeared stress-energy tensor of a full source + apparatus system.

Equality of a global Hamiltonian expectation does not establish

\[
\langle T_{\mu\nu}(x)\rangle_+=\langle T_{\mu\nu}(x)\rangle_-
\]

throughout spacetime.

This is now the main physical consistency gate.

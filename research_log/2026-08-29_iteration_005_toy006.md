## Main result

### ✅ RQIR-NG-003 — generic complete density-history tomography obstruction

For a finite one-particle `d`-mode source with Hamiltonian eigenbasis `|i>` and local modes `|a>`, define

\[
P_a(t)=e^{iHt}|a\rangle\langle a|e^{-iHt}.
\]

Under the sufficient conditions

1. all positive Bohr gaps are nondegenerate;
2. every energy pair has nonzero overlap in at least one local mode;
3. `W_ai=|V_ia|^2` has rank `d`;

the real span of all `P_a(t)` is the full Hermitian operator space:

\[
\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{P_a(t)\}=\operatorname{Herm}(d).
\]

Therefore

\[
\operatorname{Tr}[(\rho_+-\rho_-)P_a(t)]=0\;\forall a,t
\Rightarrow
\rho_+=\rho_-.
\]

A distinct state pair with the same complete local-density history and different response is impossible in this generic regime.

This is a conditional theorem, not a universal statement about all field theories or all degenerate/symmetric systems.

## Methodological upgrade

The right RQIR null-pair problem is not to force equality of mathematically complete source data. If the calibration observables are informationally complete, they already determine the entire quantum state and leave no hidden state direction for a response-only discriminator.

Define instead an operational observation space

\[
\mathcal S_M=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{M_\alpha\},
\]

with rank

\[
r_{obs}=\dim\mathcal S_M.
\]

The mean-level invisible subspace is

\[
\mathcal N_{obs}=\mathcal S_M^\perp,
\qquad
\dim\mathcal N_{obs}=d^2-r_{obs}.
\]

After adding symmetrized-noise equality operators, a response witness exists exactly when a commutator/retarded-response operator has nonzero projection into the remaining nullspace.

This turns RQIR state-pair design into an explicit inverse-tomography / experiment-design problem.
